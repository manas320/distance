How Distance is Measured

The app uses the triangle similarity (pinhole camera) principle. It looks for the largest, most prominent dark or contrasting object in the camera frame, measures its pixel width, and computes:

Distance = (Real Width × Focal Length) / Pixel Width

Tips for best accuracy:

  • Good lighting — avoid dark environments
  • Calibrate at exactly 1 metre first
  • Object should have clear contrast from background
  • Works best with the rear bumper/body of a vehicle
