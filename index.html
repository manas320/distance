How Distance is Measured

Uses pinhole camera triangle similarity. Each frame is converted to grayscale, Gaussian-blurred to remove noise, then Otsu's adaptive threshold separates the obstacle from the background. The largest foreground blob in the central scan band is found, its pixel width measured, and distance computed:

Distance = (Real Width × Focal Length) / Pixel Width

Tips for best accuracy:

  • Always Auto-Calibrate at exactly 1 metre first
  • Good lighting — avoid dark environments
  • Object must clearly contrast the background
  • Works best pointing at rear bumper of a vehicle
